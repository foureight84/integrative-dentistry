Our office

The building is part of the treatment

Newly remodeled, our ultra-modern facility is located in the Northgate area of Seattle. Many materials used in construction were influenced by the LEED certification system to foster a green environment and provide chemically and environmentally sensitive patients a reaction-free experience.

We have been told by many chronic fatigue and chemically sensitive patients that the place smells clean and they experience no reactions when coming to our office. We ask that all who enter our office refrain from wearing any fragrances out of respect for those more sensitive people.

Treatment rooms are divided by Lumicor resin panels embedded with plant material, made from renewable resources.

  1. Low VOC paints

    Used exclusively throughout our office.

  2. Bamboo, cork and marmoleum

    EcoTimber bamboo in the hallway and massage room, Expanko cork in the labs, Forbo Marmoleum in the treatment rooms.

  3. Formaldehyde-free plywood

    With the least toxic glues in cabinetry, flooring and counter surfaces.

  4. New HVAC and filtration

    All new ducting was installed to help keep our air quality fresh, clean, and healthy, with top-of-the-line air and water filtration.

  1. 3-D Galileos Cone Beam

    Images the whole skull and assesses airway, sinus, nose, teeth and jaws in three dimensions.

  2. Digital X-Rays

    A sensor in place of film means a minimal amount of radiation, and no developing chemicals.

  3. TekScan and BioPak

    Scientific recordings of your TMJ bite, head and neck, to diagnose headaches, muscle fatigue and craniofacial imbalance.

  4. CEREC CAD/CAM

    Designs and mills a biocompatible crown or onlay in the office, in one visit.

  5. Picasso soft-tissue laser

    Makes gum contouring, periodontal cleaning and cold sore treatment comfortable, with minimal bleeding.

  6. Intra-oral cameras

    Video and stills of your own mouth, so you can see what we are describing.

Your holistic dentists

Three doctors, three hygienists, one philosophy

Through extensive personal research and continuing studies, our team of doctors has created a soothing and effective dental environment that encompasses wellness, treats the issues relating to oral health, and promotes balance for your whole system.

  1. Dr. Bramley Birrer

    D.D.S. — UCLA School of Dentistry

    As my patient, you are not just another set of teeth; you are another conversation, another story, another friendship. By knowing who you are, I can know what your teeth mean to you, and this is where dentistry begins.

    Dr. Birrer received his D.D.S. from UCLA with Outstanding Achievement in Public Health, and a B.A. in Philosophy, summa cum laude, also from UCLA. He is SMART certified with the IAOMT and works in same-day all-ceramic fillings and crowns using CEREC CAD/CAM — strong, long-lasting, natural-looking restorations without metal, without BPA, and without a second appointment.

  2. Dr. Christine Cao

    D.M.D. — Tufts University School of Dental Medicine

    Born and raised in Southern California, Dr. Cao earned her doctorate from Tufts and returned to complete Invisalign certification and a General Practice Residency at Harbor-UCLA Medical Center, where her training included implant restorations and cosmetics. She volunteers at free clinic days and on mission trips, and believes every patient should be treated to her fullest potential — taking into account all their desires, capabilities and sensitivities to devise the most appropriate and individualized treatment plan.

  3. Dr. David Do

    D.D.S. — University of the Pacific

    Dr. Do graduated in 2012 from the University of the Pacific, completed implant training at the Pacific Institute in Vancouver, BC, and is versed in Botox through the American Academy of Facial Aesthetics. Described by colleagues as humble and calm, and "the kindest doctor," he is known for his spirit of compassion, always honoring his patients' needs. He is excited about practicing whole-body dentistry because he believes the right kind of care has the power to enhance both the physical and emotional aspects of health and well-being.

  4. Dr. Jeannette Daneals, ND

    Naturopathic physician — National College of Natural Medicine

    Dr. Daneals graduated from the National College of Natural Medicine in 2016 and completed a two-year residency at Cameron Wellness Center, where she became proficient at intravenous therapy and earned an advanced BHRT certification. She administers complex intravenous therapies including Extracorporeal Blood Oxygenation-Ozonation and photodynamic therapy, and has earned the nickname "the Vein Whisperer."

  5. Emily

    Emily received her Bachelor's in Biology at the University of Oregon and her Associate's in Dental Hygiene at Shoreline Community College. "I went into dental hygiene because I, myself, have dental anxiety. I want to help others overcome this common phobia and have a comfortable and fearless experience."

  6. Lydia

    Lydia is from Bellingham and received her Dental Hygiene certification at Bellingham Technical College. "I am passionate about providing high quality care for you that suits your needs while also building strong personal relationships."

  7. Morgan

    Morgan received her Bachelor of Science in Dental Hygiene from Pacific University. "While you're with me in my chair, I promise to create an environment that feels comfortable and safe for you, being mindful of those with dental anxiety."

Your hygienists

After 46 years of service and advocacy in biological dentistry, Dr. Paul Rubin has retired to focus on dental education. One of the early members of the IAOMT, he abandoned mercury amalgam in 1980, was honored as a Fellow in 1992 and a Master in 2007, and ran the first dental office in Washington State to install a mercury-capturing system.
