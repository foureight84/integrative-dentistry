What we do

Conventional dentistry, practiced biologically

Treatments in areas such as veneers, dental implants, pediatric dentistry, TMJ pain, sleep apnea, safe mercury removal, periodontology, and more, are all completed at the highest level with key components encompassing natural philosophies.

01

Safe Mercury Removal

Amalgam fillings are 50% mercury, a known neurotoxin. All of our providers use the SMART protocol, and your doctor will recommend contacting your naturopath to formulate a detoxification plan before removal begins.

02

Mercury & BPA-Free Fillings

We limit the amount of metal dentistry used and choose porcelain and composite resin materials whenever we can to fill a cavity or fix a broken tooth. The composites we use are BPA free.

03

Zirconia Implants

Pure zirconia implants offer a highly biocompatible, metal-free solution that combines excellent function with optimal, natural-looking esthetics. These all-ceramic implants form lasting attachments to both bone and soft tissue.

04

Same-Day Ceramic Crowns

Our CEREC system designs and fabricates biocompatible custom crowns and onlays in our own office. Rather than wait weeks for a restoration to arrive, we repair the tooth in a single appointment.

05

Ozone Therapy

Mixtures of oxygen and ozone introduced to living systems create a therapeutic transient oxidative burst that naturally kills bacteria, viruses, fungi, yeasts and parasites. It can be delivered in trays or as a gas.

06

Plaque Microscopy

A quick, non-invasive procedure we use on all of our patients to evaluate periodontal health. It identifies profiles of dental plaque bacteria that correlate with periodontal infection — many cases would otherwise go undetected.

07

Guided Biofilm Therapy

A systematic, predictable solution for biofilm management using AIRFLOW technology. Air, heated water and low-abrasive powder combine to break down and flush away biofilm, gently on enamel, gum tissue and restorations.

08

Airway Optimization

As much as half our population is affected by sleep-disordered breathing, and many people who suffer some form of it are completely unaware. We evaluate the airway and breathing of all patients, regardless of age.

09

TMJ Treatment

Severe headaches, jaw, neck, back and shoulder pain and a wide variety of other symptoms have been associated with Temporo-Mandibular Joint disorder, many of which improve with the right therapy for the jaw joint.

10

Platelet Rich Fibrin

PRF is a fully autologous, 100% natural blood concentrate obtained by low-speed centrifugation that accelerates wound healing, enhances tissue regeneration, eliminates infections, and reduces pain and swelling after dental surgery.

11

Laser Therapy

Laser therapy relieves pain, enhances circulation, reduces inflammation, boosts immune activity and accelerates natural regenerative healing through visible and infrared light energy.

12

Intravenous Therapy

Integrating nutrient-rich IV therapy around dental procedures facilitates tissue healing and supports liver detoxification better than oral supplementation. Administered in-house by Dr. Jeannette Daneals, ND.

Biological dentistry

Dentistry performed in concert with the biological system

Obviously, we have not placed any mercury fillings or used fluoride in our practice since 1987.

For more than 30 years, our doctors have extensively researched and practiced dentistry with a biological emphasis. Biological dentistry recognizes the links between oral health and general health and appreciates that any oral issues or materials placed in the mouth can have lasting effects on other systems in the body.

As biological dentists, we work with like-minded healthcare professionals to analyze material compatibility, provide nutritional support, and identify systemic detoxification steps to help remove toxins from the body.

Beyond the mercury issue we also address posture, opening the airway, TMJ function, sleep apnea, cranial balance, the possible effects of past dental work, issues relating to root canals and past teeth extractions, and effects of poor diet, sleep, and lifestyle. Nutrition counseling, detoxification, herbal medicine, acupuncture, massage, and homeopathy all play integral roles in our practice philosophy.
