Why we are different

A different kind of dental experience

Here, our Seattle holistic team of dentists practice dentistry from a holistic and naturopathic standpoint, carefully analyzing your system as a whole instead of a collection of different body parts.

Only biocompatible materials

We strongly believe in using only the most bio-compatible materials available to minimize your exposure to any toxins that may cause negative systemic problems. We accomplish this by offering metal-free, mercury-free, BPA-free, and fluoride-free alternatives to today's more traditional dental treatments.

Testing before placement

As an extra measure, we provide compatibility testing, attempting to identify the safest choice for each person, before placing any materials.

Safe amalgam removal

We take special precautions when removing potentially harmful amalgam fillings, implementing a safe mercury removal protocol to protect our patients. Our practice has met many patients that became sicker upon mercury removal when no precautionary steps were taken.

Coordinated with your other providers

We provide dental care alongside other medical modalities — general medicine, naturopathy, acupuncture, homeopathy, chiropractic care, nutritional counseling — and coordinate with your other care providers to improve your treatment results. One of the biggest issues in health care today is the lack of coordination between a patient's healthcare providers; we aim to bridge that gap.

We spend time listening to you — your concerns, preferences, ideas, and questions, and hold that ultimately, you are in charge of your body and your treatment.

What we do

Conventional dentistry, practiced biologically

Treatments in areas such as veneers, dental implants, pediatric dentistry, TMJ pain, sleep apnea, safe mercury removal, periodontology, and more, are all completed at the highest level with key components encompassing natural philosophies.

  1. 01

    Safe Mercury Removal

    Amalgam fillings are 50% mercury, a known neurotoxin. All of our providers use the SMART protocol, and your doctor will recommend contacting your naturopath to formulate a detoxification plan before removal begins.

  2. 02

    Mercury & BPA-Free Fillings

    We limit the amount of metal dentistry used and choose porcelain and composite resin materials whenever we can to fill a cavity or fix a broken tooth. The composites we use are BPA free.

  3. 03

    Zirconia Implants

    Pure zirconia implants offer a highly biocompatible, metal-free solution that combines excellent function with optimal, natural-looking esthetics. These all-ceramic implants form lasting attachments to both bone and soft tissue.

  4. 04

    Same-Day Ceramic Crowns

    Our CEREC system designs and fabricates biocompatible custom crowns and onlays in our own office. Rather than wait weeks for a restoration to arrive, we repair the tooth in a single appointment.

  5. 05

    Ozone Therapy

    Mixtures of oxygen and ozone introduced to living systems create a therapeutic transient oxidative burst that naturally kills bacteria, viruses, fungi, yeasts and parasites. It can be delivered in trays or as a gas.

  6. 06

    Plaque Microscopy

    A quick, non-invasive procedure we use on all of our patients to evaluate periodontal health. It identifies profiles of dental plaque bacteria that correlate with periodontal infection — many cases would otherwise go undetected.

  7. 07

    Guided Biofilm Therapy

    A systematic, predictable solution for biofilm management using AIRFLOW technology. Air, heated water and low-abrasive powder combine to break down and flush away biofilm, gently on enamel, gum tissue and restorations.

  8. 08

    Airway Optimization

    As much as half our population is affected by sleep-disordered breathing, and many people who suffer some form of it are completely unaware. We evaluate the airway and breathing of all patients, regardless of age.

  9. 09

    TMJ Treatment

    Severe headaches, jaw, neck, back and shoulder pain and a wide variety of other symptoms have been associated with Temporo-Mandibular Joint disorder, many of which improve with the right therapy for the jaw joint.

  10. 10

    Platelet Rich Fibrin

    PRF is a fully autologous, 100% natural blood concentrate obtained by low-speed centrifugation that accelerates wound healing, enhances tissue regeneration, eliminates infections, and reduces pain and swelling after dental surgery.

  11. 11

    Laser Therapy

    Laser therapy relieves pain, enhances circulation, reduces inflammation, boosts immune activity and accelerates natural regenerative healing through visible and infrared light energy.

  12. 12

    Integrating nutrient-rich IV therapy around dental procedures facilitates tissue healing and supports liver detoxification better than oral supplementation. Administered in-house by Dr. Jeannette Daneals, ND.

Biological dentistry

Dentistry performed in concert with the biological system

For more than 30 years, our doctors have extensively researched and practiced dentistry with a biological emphasis. Biological dentistry recognizes the links between oral health and general health and appreciates that any oral issues or materials placed in the mouth can have lasting effects on other systems in the body.

As biological dentists, we work with like-minded healthcare professionals to analyze material compatibility, provide nutritional support, and identify systemic detoxification steps to help remove toxins from the body.

Beyond the mercury issue we also address posture, opening the airway, TMJ function, sleep apnea, cranial balance, the possible effects of past dental work, issues relating to root canals and past teeth extractions, and effects of poor diet, sleep, and lifestyle. Nutrition counseling, detoxification, herbal medicine, acupuncture, massage, and homeopathy all play integral roles in our practice philosophy.

Obviously, we have not placed any mercury fillings or used fluoride in our practice since 1987.

Questions

The things people ask before they call

Does your office remove amalgam / mercury / silver fillings?

We do. All of our providers use the SMART protocol to safely remove these fillings. When your doctor first treatment-plans your amalgam removal, they will recommend getting in contact with your naturopath to formulate a detoxification plan. Our office also offers naturopathic IV therapy options that can be completed before or after your removal procedure.

What material does your office use for cavity fillings?

Our office uses composite materials to fill cavities. The material is BPA free. We use some of the most popular brands in holistic dentistry, including Gradia SO, Admira Fusion, and Empress.

I've been told I need a root canal. What are my options?

We can recommend time and supportive measures within our toolkit to help prolong needing the procedure and re-evaluate afterwards. If there is pain or swelling, that also factors in. We do feel that most of the time, root canals are not the best option for some people; the alternative is to extract the tooth and either place an implant or bridge. The first step is an exam to evaluate what options are available to you.

Does your office accept insurance?

Our office does accept insurance. We are out-of-network with all insurances, but we do bill insurances that accept claims from non-contracted dentists. We collect for all services performed on that day, and the insurance is asked to reimburse you back directly — which insurances tend to do when billed by an out-of-network provider.

Does your office see children?

Our office welcomes patients 13 years and older. For children under 13, we recommend seeing a pediatric dentist to ensure they receive specialized care tailored to their needs. If you are already a patient of ours and would like to keep the care for your family in one location, we can certainly accommodate that and will happily see your child in that case.

Can I get a second opinion on a treatment plan from another office?

Absolutely. You can send us a copy of your treatment plan, or give us the codes they gave you over the phone, and we can tell you the cost of the equivalent in our office. Bear in mind that unless we see you for an exam first, we cannot say that the quoted costs are final, as we have not evaluated you ourselves yet.

What are your office hours?

We are open Monday through Thursday from 7 AM to 4 PM. To see our current availability, book online with us or call us at (206) 367-6453.

I've moved away. Can you recommend a holistic dentist near me?

We are sad to see you go. The best way to find a dentist who likely shares the same holistic values we do is the Dentist Finder on IAOMT.org — there you can see the nearest dentists who follow the same protocols our office does.
